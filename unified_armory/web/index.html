Unified Armory

Wear armor from any Halo game in every Master Chief Collection campaign.

1 Pick your armor

Every piece is the real model from its game.

Different armor for a specific game

2 Apply to your games

Unified Armory adds your armor to each campaign below and installs it into MCC. Your original maps are backed up first.

    This takes a while the first time: each campaign's levels are rebuilt. Then start MCC and choose Play with mods (EAC off).
